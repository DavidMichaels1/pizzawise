# PizzaWise — user guide

PizzaWise helps you build the pizza you actually want, then finds which nearby pizzeria offers the best deal on it — instead of you opening five different apps and comparing prices yourself.

**Try it live:** https://web-production-80031.up.railway.app

## 1. Creating an account

You can browse and compare prices without an account, but you'll need one to place an order, save favorites, or view order history.

1. Click **Sign up** in the top right.
2. Enter your name, email, and a password (at least 8 characters).
3. You're automatically logged in once you sign up.

Already have an account? Click **Log in** instead and enter your email and password.

## 2. Building your pizza

From the home page, you'll go through five quick steps:

1. **Size** — Small, Medium, or Large.
2. **Crust** — Thin, Stuffed, Gluten Free, Sourdough, Pan, or Neapolitan.
3. **Sauce** — Tomato, White, Pesto, BBQ, or San Marzano.
4. **Toppings** — pick as many as you like, or none at all.
5. **Delivery location** — either click **Use my location** to share your device's location, or pick one of the preset areas from the dropdown.

As you go, the pizza illustration below the wizard updates live to match your picks. Use the arrow buttons on the sides of the card to move forward and back through the steps at any time.

Once you set your location, PizzaWise automatically starts comparing prices — there's no separate "submit" button.

## 3. Reading your results

After a few seconds, you'll see a ranked list of nearby pizzerias, alongside a picture of the pizza you just built. Here's how to read each entry:

- **Price** — what that pizzeria would charge for your exact build.
- **Distance and ETA** — how far away it is and its estimated delivery time.
- **"Closest match"** — if you see this note, it means that particular pizzeria didn't have everything you asked for (maybe a topping, your exact crust, or your sauce). It tells you exactly what's missing or substituted, and still shows you the real price for what it *can* make.

**How the ranking works:** pizzerias that can make your pizza exactly as requested always appear before ones that can't, no matter the price — a compromise isn't better value just because it's cheaper. Within each of those two groups, pizzerias are ranked by a blend of price, distance, and delivery time, so the very top result is a genuine best-value pick, not just the cheapest one on the map.

Results are shown five at a time — use the up and down arrows on the list to page through the rest.

## 4. Placing an order

Found one you like? Click **Order** on that pizzeria's card. If you're not logged in yet, you'll be asked to log in or sign up first — your pizza itself is kept exactly as you built it, so once you're in you'll just need to set your delivery location again and pick from the results, rather than rebuilding the whole thing from scratch.

Once placed, you're taken to your order's detail page, which shows a live status:

`Placed → Preparing → Out for delivery → Delivered`

This status updates automatically over time based on that pizzeria's estimated delivery time — refresh the page or check back later to see it progress. You can cancel an order from this page as long as it hasn't been delivered yet.

## 5. Saving favorites

Building the same pizza every time gets old. On the results page, click **Save as favorite** and give it a name (like "Friday night order"). 

To reuse one later:

1. Click **Favorites** in the top navigation.
2. Find the one you want and click **Use this**.
3. Since everything about the pizza is already decided, you'll be taken straight to the delivery location step — just pick where it's going, and your results will come right up.

You can delete a saved favorite from the same page at any time.

## 6. Your order history

Click **Orders** in the top navigation to see every order you've placed, each with its current status. Click into any one for the full detail view, including the option to cancel it.

## 7. Editing or restarting a pizza

On the results page:

- **Edit pizza** takes you back through the wizard with everything you picked still selected, so you can tweak just one thing (like swapping a topping) without starting over.

## Tips

- **A topping showing as "missing" everywhere nearby** doesn't necessarily mean something's wrong — some toppings are genuinely only carried by a handful of pizzerias citywide. If you widen your search area (try a different preset location), you may find more options.
- **No results at all** usually means every nearby pizzeria happened to be temporarily unavailable — this is a known, occasional hiccup with the upstream pricing service, not a sign anything is broken on your end. Try again in a moment.
